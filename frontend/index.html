    How it works

    The Musical Compass works like the political compass, except the questions are lyrics and you choose the axes.

    1. Name a feeling for each direction. A language model writes hundreds of short phrases for each word.
    2. Those phrases are turned into embeddings, and a linear classifier finds the direction that separates each pair, like Sad from Happy.
    3. Add a song. Its lyrics are looked up on LRCLIB (or pasted by you), embedded line by line, and projected onto both directions.
    4. The result is two scores from −10 to +10 and a quadrant. The sound of a song doesn't count, only its words.
